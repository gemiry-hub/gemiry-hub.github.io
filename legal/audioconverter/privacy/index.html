Privacy Policy for Audio Converter

Last updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gemiry Company Limited ("Gemiry," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with Audio Converter (the "App"), an app for converting audio files. It applies to every version of the App, including those distributed through the Mac App Store (macOS) and Google Play (Android). We built Audio Converter with privacy in mind.

1. We do not collect your information

Audio Converter does not collect, store, or share your personal information. We do not ask you to create an account, and the App does not gather your name, email address, contacts, or other personal data to send to us.

2. Your audio files stay on your device

Converting, editing, and saving audio happens entirely on your device. The App reads the files you select only to perform the conversion you asked for, and writes the result where you choose. Your audio files and their contents are never uploaded to our servers or shared with us or any third party.

Any settings or preferences you configure are kept on your device.

3. Permissions

To do its job, the App asks for access to the audio, media, or files you want to convert, and to the folder where results are saved. On macOS this is granted through the system file picker under App Sandbox; on Android it uses the system document picker or the media access permission. These permissions are used solely for conversion and are subject to your platform's own file access controls. You can revoke them at any time in your system settings, though the App will not be able to convert files without them.

4. Third-party services

The App uses third-party software development kits (SDKs) that may collect limited, anonymous technical information on their own behalf. We use these only to keep the App stable and to understand how it is used in aggregate — never to identify you.

  • Google Firebase (including Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics) — anonymous usage statistics and crash reports. This may include app events, crash diagnostics, device model, operating system version, app version, and approximate (country-level) location derived from your IP address.

This information is collected and processed by Google under its own privacy policy; see Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security. We never send your audio files, file contents, file names, or folder paths to these services.

Separately, the store you installed the App from — Apple or Google — may collect information such as downloads, purchases, and crash reports under its own privacy policy. That collection is outside our control and is not something we request or receive in identifiable form.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. The only information leaving the App is the anonymous diagnostic and usage data described above, which goes to Google as our service provider. We may also disclose information where required by law.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the GDPR (European Economic Area and United Kingdom), the CCPA/CPRA (California), and similar laws elsewhere give you the right to access, correct, delete, or export personal data a company holds about you. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to retrieve, correct, export, or delete. Requests generally concern the anonymous data held by Google as described above; you can also uninstall the App at any time to stop any further collection. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@gemiry.com.
