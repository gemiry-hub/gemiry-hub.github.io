Privacy Policy for ReScreen

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gemiry Company Limited ("Gemiry," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with ReScreen (the "App"), a Windows app that records your screen, a window or an area, with your microphone, your PC's sound and your camera, builds scenes in Studio, and can stream live to services you choose. It applies to every version of the App, including those distributed through the Microsoft Store.

1. We do not collect your information

ReScreen does not collect, store, or share your personal information. We do not ask you to create an account, and the App does not send your recordings, screenshots, scenes, file names, usage data, or any other personal data to us.

2. Screen, microphone, camera, and system sound

The App uses your screen, microphone, camera, and PC sound only for the job you asked for, and all of that work happens on your device:

  • Screen capture records only the screen, window, or area you picked, and only while a recording or stream is running. Studio also shows a live preview of your scenes while its window is open; nothing is stored until you press Record.
  • Microphone and system sound are captured only from the sources you switched on. Level meters listen to them while the App shows its mixer or audio settings, so you can check them before recording. Windows shows its microphone indicator while this happens.
  • Camera is used only when you add it to a recording or a scene, and for its preview.
  • Recordings and screenshots are saved to the folder you chose (Videos\ReScreen by default). Your scenes, settings, and a small journal used to recover a recording interrupted by a crash or power loss are kept in the App's own local storage.

Windows asks for your permission before the App can use a microphone or camera, and you can withdraw it at any time in Windows Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone or Camera.

3. Live streaming

When you go live, the App sends the picture and sound of your stream directly from your PC to the services you set up, such as YouTube, Twitch, Facebook, Kick, or a custom server you entered. It does not pass through any server of ours. What happens to your stream there is governed by that service's own privacy policy and terms.

Your stream keys are stored in the Windows Credential Locker on your PC and are sent only to the service they belong to. We never see them.

4. Network connections

ReScreen contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking software of any kind. Besides the live streams you start, it makes only the following connections:

  • Microsoft Store, on your device, to check your Pro license, to process a purchase you start, and to open the Store's review dialog if you choose to rate the App.
  • A small remote settings file. Once per launch, the App downloads a tiny settings file (a few dozen bytes, such as whether to show a rating prompt) from a Google Firebase server. The request carries no account, device, or advertising identifier and nothing about your recordings; like any web request, it does reveal your IP address to the server that answers it. The App works normally if the request fails or is blocked.
  • License keys. If you activate Pro with a license key bought on our website, the App sends that key to our payment provider, Polar (polar.sh), to check that it is valid, and checks it again from time to time. Nothing else about you or your recordings is sent. Polar handles that purchase under its own Privacy Policy.

Separately, Microsoft may collect information such as downloads, purchases, and crash reports through the Microsoft Store and Windows under its own Privacy Statement. That collection is outside our control and is not something we request or receive in identifiable form.

5. Reporting a problem

Settings > Report a problem puts a text file of the App's own logs on your clipboard and opens your email app with a message to us. Nothing is sent unless you paste the file and send that email yourself. The logs can contain device names (such as your microphone's or camera's name), the names of the services you stream to, and file paths, but never your recordings, screenshots, or stream keys. We use a report only to fix the problem it describes and delete it when it is no longer needed.

6. Recording and streaming others

What you capture is your choice and your responsibility. When your recording or stream includes other people, such as a call or a meeting, or content that belongs to someone else, you are responsible for having their consent or the rights where the law requires it.

7. Purchases

Pro is sold as Microsoft Store add-ons, and may also be sold as a license key on our website. A Store purchase is handled by the Microsoft Store using your Microsoft account; a website purchase is handled by Polar. We never see your payment details in the App. The App only learns whether you hold a valid license, which it uses to unlock Pro features.

8. Selling and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information, and because the App sends us nothing about you or your recordings, we have nothing to share. We may disclose information where required by law, though in practice we hold none.

9. Worldwide availability and your rights

The App is available worldwide, and this policy applies to you wherever you use it. Privacy laws such as the GDPR (European Economic Area and United Kingdom), the UK Data Protection Act, the CCPA/CPRA (California), the PIPL (China), and similar laws elsewhere give you rights over the personal data a company holds about you — access, correction, deletion, and portability. We hold no personal data about you, apart from any problem report you choose to email us, which we will delete on request. Your recordings, scenes, stream keys, and settings remain on your device and under your control, and you can remove them at any time by deleting them or uninstalling the App.

10. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

12. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@gemiry.com.
