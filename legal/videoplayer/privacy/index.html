Privacy Policy for Video Player

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gemiry Company Limited ("Gemiry," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with Video Player (the "App"), an app that plays video files, discs, disc images, and network streams on your PC. It applies to every version of the App, including the one distributed through the Microsoft Store. We built Video Player with privacy in mind.

1. We do not collect your information

Video Player does not collect, store, or share your personal information. We do not ask you to create an account, and the App does not send us your name, email address, videos, viewing history, or other personal data. Apart from the streams you open yourself (section 3), the App's only network connection of its own is the settings check described in section 6; it carries nothing about you or what you watch.

2. Your videos and viewing history stay on your device

The App plays the files, discs, disc images, and folders you open, on your device. It does not upload them, and it does not look up titles, artwork, or other details about them online.

To let you pick up where you left off, the App keeps a recent list and the resume position of each video in a file on your device. For a file this is its path; for a disc, its volume label and location; for a stream, its web address without any user name or password. The App also remembers its own settings, such as volume and playback options. None of this leaves your device. You can clear the recent list, together with its resume positions, at any time.

When you take a snapshot of a video frame, the picture is saved where you choose; nothing is uploaded.

3. Network streams you open

When you open a web address (for example a stream from the internet, a home media server, or a camera on your network), the App connects directly to that address to play it, the same way a web browser would. It contacts only the address you open, and that server sees what any server sees, such as your IP address, under its own privacy policy. The App does not route the stream through us and we never see it. Security certificates that cannot be verified are refused.

4. Saved stream sign-ins

If a stream asks you to sign in and you leave Remember on this PC checked, the App saves that user name and password for that server on your device only, encrypted with Windows data protection for your Windows account. Saved sign-ins are not synced to other devices, never sent to us, and never written to the App's logs, recent list, or problem reports. A saved sign-in is sent only to the server it was saved for. You can delete all saved sign-ins at any time in Settings.

5. Diagnostics and problem reports

If the App runs into an error, it writes a short diagnostic log on your device. The log contains the App and Windows versions and error details. It never contains the names or locations of your files, and for a stream it records only the kind of address (such as "https"), never the server, the rest of the address, or a user name or password.

The log never leaves your device on its own. If you choose Report a problem in Settings, the App saves the log to a file, puts that file on your clipboard, and opens a new email to us in your own email app. Nothing is sent unless you paste the file and send the email yourself. We use what you send only to investigate the problem you reported, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

6. App settings from our server

Once each time it starts, the App downloads a small settings file (under 100 bytes) from Google Firebase Realtime Database, a service Gemiry uses to host it. The file holds a few on/off switches that let us adjust, without an update, how the App asks for a rating. The request is a plain download: it sends no identifier, account, device name, or information about what you watch. Like any internet request, it reveals your IP address to the server, which Google may log under its own privacy policy; we do not use it to identify you. The App keeps a copy of the file on your device so it can start without a connection.

7. Ratings and Store links

Video Player has no in-app purchases. The App records on your device when it was first run and how often it has asked for a rating; these records never leave your device. When you choose Rate, Send feedback, or one of the other apps under More apps, the App opens the Microsoft Store or your email app, which then work under their own privacy policies.

8. No analytics, tracking, or ads

Video Player contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking software development kits (SDKs), and it does not use an advertising ID. The Microsoft Store and Firebase services above are reached through Windows and plain web requests, not through a tracking SDK.

Separately, Microsoft may collect information such as downloads and crash reports through the Microsoft Store under its own privacy policy. That collection is outside our control and is not something we request or receive in identifiable form.

9. Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information. The only information we ever receive is what you choose to email us. We may disclose information where required by law.

10. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the GDPR (European Economic Area and United Kingdom), the CCPA/CPRA (California), and similar laws elsewhere give you the right to access, correct, delete, or export personal data a company holds about you. We hold no personal data about you unless you have emailed us; in that case we will retrieve, export, or delete that email and anything attached to it on request. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

13. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@gemiry.com.
