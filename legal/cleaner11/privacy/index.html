Privacy Policy for Cleaner11

Last updated: September 3, 2026
Microsoft Store product ID: 9PB8M1TZJ8JD

This Privacy Policy explains how Gemiry Company Limited ("Gemiry," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with Cleaner11 (the "App"), a Windows app that frees disk space and clears the usage records Windows and other programs keep about you. It applies to every version of the App, including those distributed through the Microsoft Store. We built Cleaner11 with privacy in mind: a privacy tool that reported on you would be failing at its own job.

The App is identified by Microsoft Store product ID 9PB8M1TZJ8JD. That ID is what this policy refers to: if the App's display name changes, this policy continues to apply to the same product without interruption.

1. We do not collect your information

Cleaner11 does not collect, store, or share your personal information. We do not ask you to create an account, and the App does not gather your name, email address, files, file names, folder paths, browsing history, or any other personal data to send to us.

2. Everything happens on your device

The App reads your disks only to do the job you asked for, and all of that work happens entirely on your device:

  • Clean lists Windows and application caches, temporary files, and update leftovers, and removes the ones you choose.
  • Analyze measures which folders and files take up space so you can see where it went.
  • Duplicates compares the contents of files in the folders you select to find identical copies.
  • Similar reads the pixels and camera metadata of your photos to group near-identical shots.
  • Privacy counts and erases usage records — such as recent-file lists, jump lists, Run and search history, clipboard and notification history, the DNS cache, browser history, media player history, and Office recent-document lists — and lets you change the Windows settings that create those records.

Nothing the App reads, counts, compares, or erases is uploaded to our servers or shared with us or any third party. Files are deleted and records are erased only when you choose to remove them. Any settings or preferences you configure are kept on your device.

3. Backups the App keeps for you

To make its actions reversible, the App can keep copies of what it changes, and it keeps them only on your device:

  • If you choose the backup option, files the App deletes are moved to a backup folder you pick, together with a plain-text manifest so they can be restored.
  • Before it erases a usage record or changes a Windows setting stored in the registry, the App saves a copy of the previous value in its own local app-data folder so the change can be undone.

These backups never leave your device. You can empty or delete them at any time from the App or from File Explorer, and they are removed when you uninstall the App (the backup folder you picked yourself is left in place, because it is your folder).

4. Permissions and administrator access

The App runs as a normal Windows program and does not run as administrator. A few locations, such as Windows update leftovers, can only be cleaned with administrator rights; for those, and only when you start such a clean, the App launches a small helper program that shows the standard Windows User Account Control prompt. You can decline the prompt, and the rest of the clean proceeds without those locations.

Because the Privacy page reads and erases records that Windows keeps in the registry, the App declares the Microsoft Store capability that lets it read and write the real registry rather than a private copy. It uses that access only for the records and settings shown on the Privacy page, and only when you ask.

5. No analytics, tracking, or ads

Cleaner11 contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking software of any kind, and it makes no network connections of its own. The only outside service the App talks to is the Microsoft Store, on your device, to check your license, to process a purchase you start, and to open the Store's review dialog if you choose to rate the App.

Separately, Microsoft may collect information such as downloads, purchases, and crash reports through the Microsoft Store and Windows under its own Privacy Statement. That collection is outside our control and is not something we request or receive in identifiable form.

6. Purchases

Pro is sold as Microsoft Store add-ons. The purchase is handled by the Microsoft Store using your Microsoft account; we never see your payment details. The App only learns whether your account holds a valid license, which it uses to unlock Pro features.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and because nothing leaves your device, we have nothing to share. We may disclose information where required by law, though in practice we hold none.

8. Worldwide availability and your rights

The App is available worldwide, and this policy applies to you wherever you use it. Privacy laws such as the GDPR (European Economic Area and United Kingdom), the UK Data Protection Act, the CCPA/CPRA (California), the PIPL (China), and similar laws elsewhere give you rights over the personal data a company holds about you — access, correction, deletion, and portability. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to retrieve, correct, export, or delete. Your files, backups, and settings remain on your device and under your control, and you can remove them at any time by deleting them or uninstalling the App.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

11. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@gemiry.com.
