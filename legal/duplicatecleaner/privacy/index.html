Privacy Policy for Duplicate Cleaner

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gemiry Company Limited ("Gemiry," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with Duplicate Cleaner (the "App"), an Android app that finds and removes duplicate files on your device. We built Duplicate Cleaner with privacy in mind.

1. We do not collect your personal information

Duplicate Cleaner does not collect, store, or share your personal information. We do not ask you to create an account, and the App does not gather your name, contacts, or other personal data to send to us.

2. Your files stay on your device

Scanning, comparing, and deleting files happens entirely on your device. The App reads your photos, videos, audio, and other files only to detect duplicates, and nothing is uploaded to our servers or shared with any third party. Files are deleted only when you choose to delete them.

Any settings or preferences you configure are kept on your device.

3. Permissions

To do its job, the App asks for permission to access media and files on your device (for example, photos, videos, audio, or all-files access, depending on your Android version). These permissions are used solely to scan for and remove duplicates. You can revoke them at any time in Android Settings, though the App will not be able to scan without them.

4. Analytics and advertising

The App uses the following Google services, which may collect anonymous usage and device information such as app events, crash reports, device model, operating system version, approximate (country-level) location, and advertising identifiers:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — anonymous usage statistics and crash reports that help us understand how the App is used and fix problems.
  • Google AdMob — to display ads in the App. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted.

This information is collected and processed by Google under its own privacy policy. We never send your files or file contents to these services. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

You can limit ad personalization or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. The only information leaving the App is the anonymous analytics and advertising data described above, which goes to Google as our service provider. We may also disclose information where required by law.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete information relating to you. Because we do not collect personal information ourselves, such requests generally concern data held by Google; you can also uninstall the App to stop any further collection. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@gemiry.com.
