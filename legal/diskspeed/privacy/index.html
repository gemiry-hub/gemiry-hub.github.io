Privacy Policy for Disk Speed

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gemiry Company Limited ("Gemiry," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with Disk Speed (the "App"), an app that measures how fast the drives in your PC read and write. It applies to every version of the App, including the one distributed through the Microsoft Store. We built Disk Speed with privacy in mind.

1. We do not collect your information

Disk Speed does not collect, store, or share your personal information. We do not ask you to create an account, and the App does not gather your name, email address, files, or other personal data to send to us. The App makes no network connections of its own.

2. Speed tests stay on your device

Speed tests run entirely on your device. To measure a drive, the App writes a temporary test file of random data in a folder of its own on that drive (.gemiry-disk-speed) or in its own app storage, reads it back, and deletes it when the test ends. The App does not open, read, or change your own files.

To list your drives, the App reads basic facts from Windows, such as each drive's letter, type (for example NVMe, SSD, HDD, or USB), size, file system, and model name, and shows them on screen. Test results, settings, and preferences are kept on your device. When you choose Copy, the results are placed on your clipboard as text or as a picture; nothing is uploaded.

3. Diagnostics and problem reports

If the App runs into an error, it writes a short diagnostic log on your device. The log contains the App and Windows versions, error details, and drive letters with their type and file system. It does not contain drive labels, model names, serial numbers, or the names of your files.

The log never leaves your device on its own. If you choose Report a problem in Settings, the App saves the log to a file, puts that file on your clipboard, and opens a new email to us in your own email app. Nothing is sent unless you paste the file and send the email yourself. We use what you send only to investigate the problem you reported, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

4. No analytics, tracking, or ads

Disk Speed contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking software development kits (SDKs), and it does not use an advertising ID.

When you choose Rate, Send feedback, or one of the other apps under More apps, the App opens the Microsoft Store or your email app, which then work under their own privacy policies. Separately, Microsoft may collect information such as downloads, purchases, and crash reports through the Microsoft Store under its own privacy policy. That collection is outside our control and is not something we request or receive in identifiable form.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information. The only information we ever receive is what you choose to email us. We may disclose information where required by law.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the GDPR (European Economic Area and United Kingdom), the CCPA/CPRA (California), and similar laws elsewhere give you the right to access, correct, delete, or export personal data a company holds about you. We hold no personal data about you unless you have emailed us; in that case we will retrieve, export, or delete that email and anything attached to it on request. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@gemiry.com.
