Privacy Policy for PDF Toys

Last updated: August 14, 2026
Microsoft Store product ID: 9P339MCS55QX

This Privacy Policy explains how Gemiry Company Limited ("Gemiry," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with PDF Toys (the "App"), an app for working with PDF files. We built PDF Toys with privacy in mind.

The App is identified by Microsoft Store product ID 9P339MCS55QX. That ID is what this policy refers to: if the App's display name changes, this policy continues to apply to the same product without interruption.

1. We do not collect your information or files

PDF Toys does not collect, store, or share your personal information. We do not ask you to create an account, and the App does not gather your name, files, or other personal data to send to us.

The PDF files you work with are processed locally on your device and saved where you choose. They are never uploaded to our servers or shared with us or any third party.

2. Local operation

All PDF processing happens on your device. Any settings or preferences you configure are kept on your device. The App does not transmit your files or data to our servers or to any third party.

3. Document passwords

When you use a tool that adds, removes, or recovers a PDF password, that password is used only on your device for that operation. We do not collect, transmit, store, or log document passwords, and we never see them or the documents they protect.

These tools are intended only for documents you own or are authorized to access. Your responsibilities when using them are described in our Terms & Conditions.

4. No analytics, tracking, or ads

We do not use analytics or tracking, we do not show ads, and we do not sell or share your data. There is no profile or identifier associated with you.

5. Worldwide availability and your rights

The App is available worldwide, and this policy applies to you wherever you use it. Because everything stays on your device, there is no transfer of your personal data to another country, and no data for us to disclose, sell, or hand over to anyone.

Privacy laws such as the GDPR (European Economic Area and United Kingdom), the UK Data Protection Act, the CCPA/CPRA (California), the PIPL (China), and similar laws elsewhere give you rights over the personal data a company holds about you — access, correction, deletion, and portability. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to retrieve, correct, export, or delete. Your files, settings, and passwords remain on your device and under your control, and you can remove them at any time by deleting them or uninstalling the App.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

8. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@gemiry.com.
