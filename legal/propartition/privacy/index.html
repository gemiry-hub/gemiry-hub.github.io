Privacy Policy for ProPartition

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gemiry Company Limited ("Gemiry," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with ProPartition (the "App"), a disk partition manager for Windows that resizes, creates, moves, and recovers partitions, checks drive health, clones disks, moves Windows to another disk, and converts disks from MBR to GPT. It applies to every version of the App, including the one distributed through the Microsoft Store. We built ProPartition with privacy in mind.

1. We do not collect your information

ProPartition does not collect, store, or share your personal information. We do not ask you to create an account, and the App does not gather your name, email address, files, or other personal data to send to us. The App's only network connection of its own is the settings check described in section 4; it carries nothing about you or your disks.

2. Disk and partition information stays on your device

To show and change your disks, the App reads from Windows and from the disks themselves: disk models, sizes, and connection types, partition tables, volume labels, drive letters, file systems, BitLocker state, and S.M.A.R.T. health data. When you scan for lost partitions, it reads the disk to find them and can show the file and folder names inside a partition it finds, so you can tell which one to restore. Changes you apply, such as a resize, move, clone, recovery, or MBR-to-GPT conversion, are carried out on your PC by a helper that Windows asks you to allow with administrator rights. Copies made by Clone or by moving Windows go only to the disk you choose in your PC.

All of this stays on your device. So that an interrupted change can be finished or undone safely, the App keeps records of changes in progress, of clones and Windows moves, and backup copies of the partition tables it rewrites, in its folder under ProgramData on your PC. It also keeps an operation log of the changes you apply, which names your disks, volume labels, and drive letters, and its own settings, such as your language choice. When you export the log, it is saved only where you choose; nothing is uploaded.

3. Diagnostics and problem reports

If the App runs into an error, it writes a short diagnostic log on your device with the App and Windows versions and the error details. An error message can include a file path, and a Windows path contains your user name.

These logs never leave your device on their own. If you choose Report a problem in Settings, the App gathers the diagnostic log and the two most recent operation logs into one plain text file you can read first, puts that file on your clipboard, and opens a new email to feedback@gemiry.com in your own email app. Nothing is sent unless you paste the file and send the email yourself. We use what you send only to investigate the problem you reported, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

4. App settings from our server

Once each time it starts, the App downloads a small settings file (under 100 bytes) from Google Firebase Realtime Database, a service Gemiry uses to host it. The file holds a few on/off switches that let us adjust, without an update, how the App asks for a rating and how the upgrade window shows prices. The request is a plain download: it sends no identifier, account, device name, or disk information. Like any internet request, it reveals your IP address to the server, which Google may log under its own privacy policy; we do not use it to identify you. The App keeps a copy of the file on your device so it can start without a connection.

5. Purchases and ratings

ProPartition Pro is an optional one-time purchase made through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft handles the payment and your account; the App only asks the Store whether this Microsoft account owns Pro, and keeps that answer on your device so Pro still works when you are offline. We do not receive your payment details. The App also records on your device when it was first run and how often it has asked for a rating; these records never leave your device.

From time to time, including when you close it, the App may ask whether you would like to rate it. When you choose to rate it, or choose Rate, Send feedback, Upgrade, redeem a code, or one of the other apps under More apps, the App opens the Microsoft Store or your email app, which then work under their own privacy policies.

6. No analytics, tracking, or ads

ProPartition contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking software development kits (SDKs), and it does not use an advertising ID. The Microsoft Store and Firebase services above are reached through Windows and plain web requests, not through a tracking SDK.

Separately, Microsoft may collect information such as downloads, purchases, and crash reports through the Microsoft Store under its own privacy policy. That collection is outside our control and is not something we request or receive in identifiable form.

7. Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information. The only information we ever receive is what you choose to email us. We may disclose information where required by law.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the GDPR (European Economic Area and United Kingdom), the CCPA/CPRA (California), and similar laws elsewhere give you the right to access, correct, delete, or export personal data a company holds about you. We hold no personal data about you unless you have emailed us; in that case we will retrieve, export, or delete that email and anything attached to it on request. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

11. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@gemiry.com.
