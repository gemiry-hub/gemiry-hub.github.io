Privacy Policy for ProRip

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gemiry Company Limited ("Gemiry," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with ProRip (the "App"), a Windows app that copies audio CDs to files, looks up album and track information, checks the copy against online databases of verified rips, and tags the files it writes. It applies to every version of the App, including those distributed through the Microsoft Store.

1. We do not collect your information

ProRip does not collect, store, or share your personal information. We do not ask you to create an account, and the App does not send us your name, email address, music, file names, folder paths, or any other personal data. The only way information about you reaches us is if you choose to email us, including through "Report a problem" (see section 5).

2. Your CDs and files stay on your device

The App reads the audio from the CD in your drive, or from a disc image you open, and writes the files to the folder you choose. Reading, encoding, and tagging all happen entirely on your device. No audio is ever uploaded to us or to anyone else. On your device the App keeps:

  • The files you rip, together with the rip log, CUE sheet, and cover art file it writes next to them if those options are on. They are saved only where you choose. The rip log records the drive, the settings used, the album details, the file names, and the verification results, as rip logs normally do.
  • Your settings, including each drive's read offset and the album match you told the App to remember for a disc.
  • Caches of recent album lookups and of AccurateRip's public list of drive offsets, so the same information is not downloaded again.
  • A crash log. So that a crash can be explained, the App keeps a small diagnostic log in its own local folder: the App and Windows versions, how far startup got, and the errors it met. An error message can contain a file path, and a Windows path can contain your Windows user name. The log stays on your device unless you send it to us (see section 5).

Everything above is removed when you uninstall the App, except the files you ripped, which are yours and stay where you saved them.

3. Online disc lookups

When you insert a CD or open a disc image, the App identifies the disc by its table of contents, the list of track start positions that every audio CD carries, and uses it to query these free, public music services, which are run by third parties and not by us:

  • MusicBrainz (musicbrainz.org) and GnuDB (gnudb.org), for the album, artist, and track names.
  • Cover Art Archive (coverartarchive.org), for the album cover of the release that was found.
  • AccurateRip (accuraterip.com) and the CUETools Database (cue.tools), to check your copy against rips of the same disc made by other people, and to download AccurateRip's public list of drive read offsets. Each can be turned off in Settings.

These requests contain the disc's table of contents or identifiers calculated from it, and name the App and its version as those services require. They do not contain your name, account, any device or advertising identifier, your audio, or anything about your other files. The App only downloads from these services; it does not submit your rips or checksums to them. Like any web request, a lookup reveals your IP address and which CD you looked up to the service that answers it, and each service handles that under its own privacy policy. If a request fails or you are offline, the App still rips, and you can type the album details yourself.

If a disc is not found, Submit to MusicBrainz opens the MusicBrainz website in your browser with the disc's table of contents filled in. Anything you add there is between you and MusicBrainz.

4. File access and permissions

The App runs as a normal Windows program so that it can read audio CDs directly from your optical drive, which is why its Microsoft Store listing shows that it uses all system resources. It reads the drive only while it identifies or rips a disc, writes only where you tell it to, and does not ask for administrator rights.

5. Network connections and "Report a problem"

ProRip contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking software of any kind. Besides the disc lookups in section 3, it connects only to the Microsoft Store, on your device, to check your Pro license, to process a purchase you start, and to open the Store's review dialog if you choose to rate the App.

Report a problem in Settings does not send anything by itself. It prepares a text file containing the crash log described in section 2, copies it to your clipboard, and opens a new email to feedback@gemiry.com in your own email app. The file is plain text so you can read it before sending. Nothing reaches us unless you paste it and send the email. If you do, we receive that email, your email address, and the log, and we use them only to answer you and to diagnose and fix the problem. We keep them no longer than needed for that, and we delete them on request.

Separately, Microsoft may collect information such as downloads, purchases, and crash reports through the Microsoft Store and Windows under its own Privacy Statement. That collection is outside our control and is not something we request or receive in identifiable form.

6. Purchases

Pro is sold through the Microsoft Store as a subscription or a one-time lifetime add-on. The purchase is handled by the Microsoft Store using your Microsoft account; we never see your payment details. The App only learns whether your account holds a valid license, which it uses to unlock Pro features. You manage or cancel a subscription in your Microsoft account.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and because the App sends us nothing about you or your music, we have nothing to share. An email you choose to send us is used only as described in section 5. We may disclose information where required by law, though in practice we hold none beyond such emails.

8. Worldwide availability and your rights

The App is available worldwide, and this policy applies to you wherever you use it. Privacy laws such as the GDPR (European Economic Area and United Kingdom), the UK Data Protection Act, the CCPA/CPRA (California), the PIPL (China), and similar laws elsewhere give you rights over the personal data a company holds about you — access, correction, deletion, and portability. Apart from any email you have sent us, we hold no personal data about you. To exercise these rights over an email you sent, contact us at the address below. Your rips, logs, and settings remain on your device and under your control, and you can remove them at any time by deleting them or uninstalling the App.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

11. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@gemiry.com.
