Privacy Policy for Disk Info

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gemiry Company Limited ("Gemiry," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with Disk Info (the "App"), an app that shows the health, temperature, wear, and details of the drives in your PC. It applies to every version of the App, including the one distributed through the Microsoft Store. We built Disk Info with privacy in mind.

1. We do not collect your information

Disk Info does not collect, store, or share your personal information. We do not ask you to create an account, and the App does not gather your name, email address, files, or other personal data to send to us. The App's only network connection of its own is the settings check described in section 4; it carries nothing about you or your drives.

2. Drive information stays on your device

To show your drives, the App asks Windows for information each drive reports about itself: its model, size, connection type, firmware version, serial number, supported features (such as TRIM), and its S.M.A.R.T. health data, including temperature, wear, and error counts. The App only reads this information. It never writes to a drive, never opens, reads, or changes your files, and does not need administrator rights.

All of this is shown on screen and stays on your device. The short temperature history is kept in memory while the App is open and is discarded when you close it. The App remembers only its window position and size, which detail sections you left open, and the purchase and rating details in section 5. When you choose Copy details, a text report of the selected drive, including its serial number, is placed on your clipboard; nothing is uploaded.

3. Diagnostics and problem reports

If the App runs into an error, it writes a short diagnostic log on your device. The log contains the App and Windows versions, error details, and, for a drive that could not be read, its disk number, connection type, and model name. It never contains a drive's serial number, drive labels, or the names of your files.

The log never leaves your device on its own. If you choose Report a problem in Settings, the App saves the log to a file, puts that file on your clipboard, and opens a new email to us in your own email app. Nothing is sent unless you paste the file and send the email yourself. We use what you send only to investigate the problem you reported, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

4. App settings from our server

Once each time it starts, the App downloads a small settings file (under 100 bytes) from Google Firebase Realtime Database, a service Gemiry uses to host it. The file holds a few on/off switches that let us adjust, without an update, how the App asks for a rating and how the upgrade window shows prices. The request is a plain download: it sends no identifier, account, device name, or drive information. Like any internet request, it reveals your IP address to the server, which Google may log under its own privacy policy; we do not use it to identify you. The App keeps a copy of the file on your device so it can start without a connection.

5. Purchases and ratings

Disk Info Pro is an optional one-time purchase made through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft handles the payment and your account; the App only asks the Store whether this Microsoft account owns Pro, and keeps that answer on your device so Pro still works when you are offline. We do not receive your payment details. The App also records on your device when it was first run and how often it has asked for a rating; these records never leave your device.

When you choose Rate, Send feedback, Upgrade, Restore purchases, or one of the other apps under More apps, the App opens the Microsoft Store or your email app, which then work under their own privacy policies.

6. No analytics, tracking, or ads

Disk Info contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking software development kits (SDKs), and it does not use an advertising ID. The Microsoft Store and Firebase services above are reached through Windows and plain web requests, not through a tracking SDK.

Separately, Microsoft may collect information such as downloads, purchases, and crash reports through the Microsoft Store under its own privacy policy. That collection is outside our control and is not something we request or receive in identifiable form.

7. Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information. The only information we ever receive is what you choose to email us. We may disclose information where required by law.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the GDPR (European Economic Area and United Kingdom), the CCPA/CPRA (California), and similar laws elsewhere give you the right to access, correct, delete, or export personal data a company holds about you. We hold no personal data about you unless you have emailed us; in that case we will retrieve, export, or delete that email and anything attached to it on request. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

11. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@gemiry.com.
