Privacy Policy for Readd

Last updated: October 30, 2026
Microsoft Store product ID: 9PFWBTBGR56N

This Privacy Policy explains how Gemiry Company Limited ("Gemiry," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with Readd, listed in the Microsoft Store as PDF Reader | PDF Editor ("Readd" or the "App"), a Windows app for reading, filling, signing, annotating, and editing PDF documents. It applies to every version of the App, including those distributed through the Microsoft Store. Your documents are your own, and Readd works on them without sending them anywhere.

The App is identified by Microsoft Store product ID 9PFWBTBGR56N. That ID is what this policy refers to: if the App's display name changes, this policy continues to apply to the same product without interruption.

1. We do not collect your information

Readd does not collect, store, or share your personal information. We do not ask you to create an account, and the App does not send us your name, email address, documents, their contents, file names, folder paths, signatures, or any other personal data. The only way information about you reaches us is if you choose to email us, including through "Report a problem" (see section 5).

2. Your documents stay on your device

Everything the App does with your documents happens entirely on your device:

  • Opening and reading reads only the files you choose or open with the App. If a document is password-protected, the password you type is used to open it and is not stored.
  • Editing, annotating, filling forms, signing, and organizing pages change the document on your device and write it only where you save it.
  • Redact removes the content you select from the file itself when you save; it is not uploaded or kept anywhere else.
  • Read text (text recognition for scanned pages) uses the text recognition built into Windows, on your device. Page images are not sent anywhere.
  • Checking digital signatures uses the certificates in the document and the trusted certificates already on your PC. The check runs offline: the App does not download certificates or look up revocation lists.
  • Print hands the pages to Windows' own print dialog and the printer you choose.

Nothing the App opens, edits, or saves is uploaded to our servers or shared with us or any third party.

3. What the App keeps on your device

  • Settings. Your preferences, the colours and sizes you chose for each tool, and the window's size and position.
  • Recent and pinned files. A short list of the documents you opened recently or pinned (their file paths), so you can open them again. You can clear the list in the App.
  • Saved signatures. Signatures you choose to save, drawn, typed, or added as a picture, so you can place them again. You can delete them in the App.
  • A copy of a Windows font. So that text you add to a page looks the same as it will in the saved file, the App keeps a copy of Windows' own Arial font in its local folder.
  • A crash log. The App and Windows versions, how far startup got, and the errors it met. An error message can include a file path, and a Windows file path usually contains your Windows user name. It never contains your documents.

All of this stays in the App's own local folder on your device unless you send the log to us (see section 5), and it is removed when you uninstall the App.

4. Permissions

Readd runs as a normal desktop program and does not ask for administrator rights. It reads the files you choose; it does not use your camera, microphone, location, or contacts.

5. Network connections and "Report a problem"

Readd contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking software of any kind. Its only connections are:

  • The Microsoft Store, on your device, to check your Pro license, to process a purchase you start, to open the Store's review dialog if you choose to rate the App, and to show a Store page when you choose to (for example our other apps, or our PDF tools app for merging and splitting files).
  • A small remote settings file. Once per launch, the App downloads a tiny settings file (a few dozen bytes, such as whether to show a rating prompt) from a Google Firebase server. The request carries no account, device, or advertising identifier and nothing about your documents; like any web request, it does reveal your IP address to the server that answers it. The App works normally if the request fails or is blocked.
  • Links in a document. A web or email link inside a PDF opens in your own browser or email app, and only when you click it. What happens then is governed by that website or service.

Report a problem in Settings does not send anything by itself. It prepares a text file containing the crash log described in section 3, together with the App version, your Windows version and language, and whether you use Pro. It copies the file to your clipboard and opens a new email to feedback@gemiry.com in your own email app. The file holds no documents, and you can read all of it before sending. Nothing reaches us unless you paste it and send the email. If you do, we receive that email, your email address, and the file, and we use them only to answer you and to diagnose and fix the problem. We keep them no longer than needed for that, and we delete them on request.

Separately, Microsoft may collect information such as downloads, purchases, and crash reports through the Microsoft Store and Windows under its own Privacy Statement. That collection is outside our control and is not something we request or receive in identifiable form.

6. Purchases

Pro is sold through the Microsoft Store as a monthly or yearly subscription or as a one-time lifetime purchase. The purchase is handled by the Microsoft Store using your Microsoft account; we never see your payment details. The App only learns whether your account holds a valid license, which it uses to unlock Pro features. You manage or cancel a subscription in your Microsoft account.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and because the App sends us nothing about you or your documents, we have nothing to share. An email you choose to send us is used only as described in section 5. We may disclose information where required by law, though in practice we hold none beyond such emails.

8. Worldwide availability and your rights

The App is available worldwide, and this policy applies to you wherever you use it. Privacy laws such as the GDPR (European Economic Area and United Kingdom), the UK Data Protection Act, the CCPA/CPRA (California), the PIPL (China), and similar laws elsewhere give you rights over the personal data a company holds about you — access, correction, deletion, and portability. Apart from any email you have sent us, we hold no personal data about you. To exercise these rights over an email you sent, contact us at the address below. Your documents, signatures, logs, and settings remain on your device and under your control, and you can remove them at any time by deleting them or uninstalling the App.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

11. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@gemiry.com.
