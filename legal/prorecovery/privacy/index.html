Privacy Policy for ProRecovery

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gemiry Company Limited ("Gemiry," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with ProRecovery (the "App"), a Windows app that scans drives, folders, and disk images for deleted and lost files and saves copies of the ones it finds. It applies to every version of the App, including those distributed through the Microsoft Store. A recovery tool reads more of your drive than almost any other app, so it matters that none of what it reads leaves your computer, and ProRecovery is built that way.

1. We do not collect your information

ProRecovery does not collect, store, or share your personal information. We do not ask you to create an account, and the App does not send us your name, email address, files, file names, folder paths, scan results, or any other personal data. The only way information about you reaches us is if you choose to email us.

2. Everything happens on your device

The App reads your drives only to do the job you asked for, and all of that work happens entirely on your device:

  • Scans read the drive, folder, or disk image you choose. A quick scan reads the file system's records of deleted files; a deep scan reads the whole drive, including space that is no longer assigned to any file. This can surface the names, locations, dates, and contents of files that you, or anyone else who used the drive, deleted in the past.
  • Recycle Bin and restore points. The App lists the items in your Recycle Bin, and it can look through restore points and other snapshots Windows keeps for files that are no longer on the drive.
  • Previews of images, text, and PDF files are produced on your device from the data the scan found, and shown only to you.
  • Recovery writes copies of the files you select to the folder you choose. The App does not write to the drive it is recovering from.
  • Lost-partition search and disk imaging read a whole disk. A disk image is saved only where you choose, together with a list of any ranges that could not be read. An image is a complete copy of the drive, including deleted data, so treat it with the same care as the drive itself.

Nothing the App reads, finds, previews, or recovers is uploaded to our servers or shared with us or any third party.

3. Administrator access and the scan helper

Windows only lets a program with administrator rights read a drive directly, which is what finding deleted files requires. When a scan needs it, the App asks your permission and starts a small scan helper with administrator rights; Windows shows its own prompt each time. The helper is part of the App. It reads from the drive being scanned and never writes to it, it talks only to the App on your own computer, and it closes when you close the App. If you decline, the App can still scan your Recycle Bin.

4. What the App keeps on your device

  • Your settings — the theme, the default recovery folder, whether to recreate the original folders, whether the preview pane is open, and whether you have seen the introduction — and a count of how often you have used the App, which decides when it may ask you for a rating. These are kept in the App's own local storage and removed when you uninstall it.
  • Scan results are held in memory while the App is open and are discarded when you start a new scan or close it. The App keeps no history of what you scanned or recovered.
  • Recovered files and disk images stay where you saved them. They are yours; the App does not track or keep copies of them.
  • Archive passwords. If an encrypted archive was found in pieces, the App may ask for its password so it can put the archive back together. The password is used on your device for that one recovery and is not saved or sent anywhere.

5. Network connections

ProRecovery contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking software of any kind, and it does not contact any server of ours. The only connections it makes are to the Microsoft Store, through Windows on your device, to check your Pro license, to process a purchase you start, and to open the Store's review dialog if you choose to rate the App.

Separately, Microsoft may collect information such as downloads, purchases, and crash reports through the Microsoft Store and Windows under its own Privacy Statement. That collection is outside our control and is not something we request or receive in identifiable form.

6. Purchases

Pro is sold as a Microsoft Store add-on. The purchase is handled by the Microsoft Store using your Microsoft account; we never see your payment details. The App only learns whether your account holds a valid license, which it uses to unlock Pro features.

7. Other people's data

A drive can hold deleted files that belonged to someone else — a previous owner, a family member, a colleague, or a customer. The App cannot tell whose a file was, and it shows whatever it finds to the person running it. What you do with files that are not yours is your responsibility under the privacy and data-protection laws that apply to you; see our Terms & Conditions.

8. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and because the App sends us nothing about you or your files, we have nothing to share. If you email us, we use your message and email address only to answer you, keep them no longer than needed for that, and delete them on request. We may disclose information where required by law, though in practice we hold none beyond such emails.

9. Worldwide availability and your rights

The App is available worldwide, and this policy applies to you wherever you use it. Privacy laws such as the GDPR (European Economic Area and United Kingdom), the UK Data Protection Act, the CCPA/CPRA (California), the PIPL (China), and similar laws elsewhere give you rights over the personal data a company holds about you — access, correction, deletion, and portability. Apart from any email you have sent us, we hold no personal data about you. To exercise these rights over an email you sent, contact us at the address below. Your recovered files, disk images, and settings remain on your device and under your control, and you can remove them at any time by deleting them or uninstalling the App.

10. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

12. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@gemiry.com.
