Privacy Policy for StickNote

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gemiry Company Limited ("Gemiry," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with StickNote (the "App"), a Windows app for sticky notes that stay on your desktop, with checklists, pictures, sketches, colors, and reminders. It applies to every version of the App, including those distributed through the Microsoft Store. Notes often hold things you would not show anyone, so the App keeps them on your computer and never sends them to us.

1. We do not collect your information

StickNote does not collect, store, or share your personal information. We do not ask you to create an account, and the App does not send us your name, email address, notes, pictures, sketches, reminders, or any other personal data. The only way information about you reaches us is if you choose to email us.

2. Your notes stay on your device

  • Notes — their text, formatting, checklists, colors, pictures, sketches, and reminder times — are saved as files in the App's own folder on your computer. Notes you delete go to the App's trash first, also on your computer, until you empty it or restore them.
  • Pictures you paste or drop into a note are copied into the note on your device. The App does not upload them anywhere.
  • Links you paste keep the address they point to. The App does not visit a link or fetch anything from it; a link opens in your browser only when you click it.
  • Reminders are shown by Windows as notifications on your computer, at the time you set. They are scheduled locally and do not pass through any server of ours.
  • Export and import. When you export notes, the App writes a text, RTF, or backup file to the place you choose. When you import, it reads only the files you pick. What you do with an exported file afterwards is up to you.

3. Sync folder (optional)

You can choose a folder for the App to keep your notes in, so that a sync program you already use — such as OneDrive, Dropbox, or Google Drive — copies them to your other PCs. The App itself only reads and writes files in that folder; it does not connect to any sync service. Whether and where those files are copied is decided by the sync program you use and is governed by its provider's own privacy policy, not by us. Window positions stay on each PC and are not put in the sync folder.

4. What the App keeps on your device

  • Your settings — keyboard shortcuts you changed, the default note color, the sort order of the notes list, snapping, the App's language, the sync folder, and where the notes list window was left — and a count of how often you have used the App, which decides when it may ask you for a rating. These are kept in the App's own local storage.
  • A diagnostics log of the App's own events and errors, used to find the cause of a problem. It stays on your device and is not sent anywhere automatically (see section 6).

Uninstalling the App removes its own folder, including notes kept there. Notes in a sync folder you chose, and files you exported, are not removed, because they are outside the App's folder.

5. Network connections

StickNote contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind. The only connections it makes are:

  • The Microsoft Store, through Windows on your device, to check your Pro license, to process a purchase you start, and to open the Store's review dialog if you choose to rate the App.
  • A small remote settings file. Once per launch, the App downloads a tiny settings file (a few dozen bytes, such as how often to show a rating prompt) from a Google Firebase server. The request carries no account, device, or advertising identifier and nothing about your notes; like any web request, it does reveal your IP address to the server that answers it. The App works normally if the request fails or is blocked.

Separately, Microsoft may collect information such as downloads, purchases, and crash reports through the Microsoft Store and Windows under its own Privacy Statement. That collection is outside our control and is not something we request or receive in identifiable form.

6. Feedback and problem reports

"Send feedback" and "Report a problem" in Settings open a new email in your own mail program. A problem report is pre-filled with the App's version, your Windows version, and the most recent lines of the diagnostics log, which can include things such as the path of your sync folder. Nothing is sent until you read the email and send it yourself, and you can edit or remove anything in it first.

7. Purchases

Pro is sold as a one-time Microsoft Store add-on. The purchase is handled by the Microsoft Store using your Microsoft account; we never see your payment details. The App only learns whether your account holds a valid license, which it uses to unlock Pro features.

8. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and because the App sends us nothing about you or your notes, we have nothing to share. If you email us, we use your message and email address only to answer you, keep them no longer than needed for that, and delete them on request. We may disclose information where required by law, though in practice we hold none beyond such emails.

9. Worldwide availability and your rights

The App is available worldwide, and this policy applies to you wherever you use it. Privacy laws such as the GDPR (European Economic Area and United Kingdom), the UK Data Protection Act, the CCPA/CPRA (California), the PIPL (China), and similar laws elsewhere give you rights over the personal data a company holds about you — access, correction, deletion, and portability. Apart from any email you have sent us, we hold no personal data about you. To exercise these rights over an email you sent, contact us at the address below. Your notes and settings remain on your device and under your control, and you can export, delete, or remove them at any time.

10. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

12. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@gemiry.com.
