Privacy Policy for Authik

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gemiry Company Limited ("Gemiry," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with Authik (the "App"), a two-factor authentication (2FA) app that generates one-time passwords (OTPs). Authik is built to keep your security data private and entirely on your device.

1. We do not collect your information

Authik does not collect, store, or share any personal information. We do not ask you to create an account, and the App never transmits your keys, accounts, or files to us or to anyone else. The only thing that leaves your device is the anonymous usage data described in section 4.

2. Your 2FA keys stay on your device

The secret keys you add to Authik are stored encrypted, locally on your device. They are never sent to our servers or to any third party. One-time passwords (OTPs) are generated on your device and do not require an internet connection.

3. Camera and QR codes

If you use the App's camera to scan a QR code when adding an account, the image is processed on your device only. Authik does not store photos or transmit camera images anywhere.

4. Third-party SDKs and analytics

Authik does not collect personal information itself, but it includes third-party software development kits (SDKs) — in particular Google Analytics for Firebase — that may collect information about your activity in the App on our behalf, for analytics only. We use this to understand how the App is used in aggregate and to keep it stable — never to identify you, and never for advertising.

In the terms used by the app stores' data-safety labels, the SDK may collect the following, all for the purpose of analytics:

Data category Data type Purpose
App activity App interactions (which features are used, app opens) Analytics
Device or other IDs A random, per-install app instance ID Analytics
Location Approximate (country-level) location derived from IP address Analytics
Financial info Purchase history (that an in-app purchase happened; no payment details) Analytics

What is never sent — your 2FA secret keys, account names, one-time passwords, or camera images. This information is collected and processed by Google as our service provider, under its own privacy policy; see Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security. This collection is optional: you can turn it off at any time in the App under Settings → Share anonymous usage statistics, and the App works exactly the same with it off. Uninstalling the App also stops any further collection.

Separately, the store you installed the App from may collect information such as downloads, purchases, and crash reports under its own privacy policy. That collection is outside our control and is not something we request or receive in identifiable form.

5. Your responsibility for backups

Because your keys are stored only on your device, you are responsible for keeping access to them. If you use any backup or export feature provided by the App or your operating system, please keep those backups secure.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

8. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@gemiry.com.
