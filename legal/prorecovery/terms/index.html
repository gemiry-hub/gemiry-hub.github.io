3. Recovery is not guaranteed

No software can promise to bring a deleted file back, and the App does not. Whether a file can be recovered depends on what has happened to the drive since the file was lost, which is outside the App's and our control. In particular:

  • On an SSD with TRIM, the drive itself clears deleted data, often within minutes. Cleared data reads back as zeros and cannot be recovered by any app.
  • Data that has been overwritten by newer files, or destroyed by a file shredder, is gone.
  • A file can be recovered in part: it may open with missing or damaged sections, or not open at all.
  • Files rebuilt by a deep scan from their content alone may come back without their original names, folders, or dates.
  • Encrypted archives found in pieces may need their password to be put back together, and drives locked by BitLocker must be unlocked before they can be scanned.

The App shows an estimate of each file's chances and, where it can, a preview. These are the App's best reading of what is left on the drive, not a promise: a file rated likely to recover can still turn out to be damaged, and the list of files found is not a guarantee that any of them can be saved intact.

6. Failing drives and disk images

The App is software. It cannot repair a drive that is physically failing, and reading such a drive — by scanning it or by copying it into a disk image — puts it under load and can make its condition worse or use up what life it has left. Parts of a failing drive that cannot be read are filled with zeros in the image and listed beside it; an image is only as complete as the drive allowed. If the data is valuable and the drive is clicking, disappearing, or reporting errors, stop and consult a professional data-recovery service before running any software on it, including this App.
