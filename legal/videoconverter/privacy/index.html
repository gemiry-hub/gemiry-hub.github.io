Privacy Policy for Any Video Converter

Last updated: August 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gemiry Company Limited ("Gemiry," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with Any Video Converter (the "App"), an app for converting video files. It applies to every version of the App distributed through the Mac App Store (macOS).

In short: your video files never leave your Mac, and we do not collect personal information about you. The App does send anonymous usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase so we can see which formats people convert to and whether conversions succeed. That is on by default and you can turn it off — section 4 explains what is sent and how to stop it.

1. We do not collect personal information

We do not collect, store, or share your personal information. We do not ask you to create an account, and the App does not gather your name, email address, contacts, or other personal data to send to us. We have no database of users and no way to identify you.

The one thing the App does send is the anonymous, aggregate usage data described in section 4. It is not tied to your identity, and it never contains anything about the files you convert.

2. Your video files stay on your Mac

Converting, trimming, and saving video happens entirely on your Mac. The App reads the files you select only to perform the conversion you asked for, and writes the result where you choose. Your videos and their contents are never uploaded to our servers or shared with us or any third party.

This is also true of everything about your files. We never send file names, folder names, file paths, video titles, embedded metadata, subtitle text, chapter names, thumbnails, or file durations. Nothing that identifies a specific file leaves your Mac.

Any settings or preferences you configure are kept on your device.

3. Permissions

To do its job, the App asks for access to the videos you want to convert and to the folder where results are saved. This is granted through the system file picker under App Sandbox, is used solely for conversion, and is subject to macOS's own file access controls. You can revoke access at any time in System Settings, though the App will not be able to convert files without it.

The App's network access is used for one purpose only — sending the usage statistics in section 4 — and for nothing else. There is no cloud conversion, no remote processing, and no way to load video from a URL or a stream.

4. Analytics, and how to turn it off

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to collect limited, anonymous usage statistics. We use these only to understand how the App is used in aggregate — which output formats and codecs matter, and whether conversions are succeeding — never to identify you.

In the terms used by the app stores' data-safety labels, the SDK may collect the following, all for the purpose of analytics:

Data category Data type Purpose
App activity App interactions (which features are used, app opens) Analytics
Device or other IDs A random, per-install app instance ID Analytics
Location Approximate (country-level) location derived from IP address Analytics
Financial info Purchase history (that an in-app purchase happened; no payment details) Analytics

What is sent. Two events of our own:

  • when you start a conversion — the output format, codec, and quality setting, and a rough size band for the batch (for example "2–5 files" rather than the exact number);
  • when a batch finishes — the output format, and how many files succeeded and failed.

Alongside those, Firebase collects a standard set of its own signals: app opens and sessions, app version, device model, operating system version, and approximate (country-level) location derived from your IP address. It also assigns a random app instance ID to your installation. That ID is per-install, not per-person: removing the App's data or reinstalling it generates a new one.

If you buy Any Video Converter Pro, Firebase also records that a purchase or subscription took place, along with the product and its price. The analytics SDK measures this automatically alongside Apple's own billing. It tells us that a purchase happened, not who made it, and it carries no payment details — we never see or receive your card, bank, or Apple Account information.

What is never sent — your videos, any part of their contents, file names, folder paths, embedded metadata, subtitle text, or thumbnails. The App also does not use an advertising ID.

How to turn it off. Open Settings › General and switch off Share anonymous usage data. Collection is on by default, so it continues until you switch it off. Once you do, the App stops sending both its own events and Firebase's automatic ones. Turning it off stops any future collection; it does not retract data that was already sent before you switched it off. You can also uninstall the App at any time, which stops collection entirely.

This information is collected and processed by Google as our service provider, under its own privacy policy; see Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security.

We do not use crash reporting, advertising, or attribution SDKs, and the App contains no ads.

Separately, Apple may collect information such as downloads, purchases, and crash reports under its own privacy policy. That collection is outside our control and is not something we request or receive in identifiable form.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. The only information leaving the App is the anonymous usage data described above, which goes to Google as our service provider. We may also disclose information where required by law.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the GDPR (European Economic Area and United Kingdom), the CCPA/CPRA (California), and similar laws elsewhere give you the right to access, correct, delete, or export personal data a company holds about you.

We hold no personal data about you, so in most cases there is nothing for us to retrieve, correct, export, or delete. Requests generally concern the anonymous analytics data held by Google as described in section 4. You can act on that directly and immediately: switch off Share anonymous usage data in the App's General settings to stop further collection, and reinstall the App to reset the app instance ID that past data was associated with. To make a request of us, or to ask us to approach Google on your behalf, contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@gemiry.com.
