Privacy Policy for ProShred

Last updated: September 28, 2026
Microsoft Store product ID: 9N4LPVVTS7RS

This Privacy Policy explains how Gemiry Company Limited ("Gemiry," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with ProShred (the "App"), a Windows app that destroys files, folders, Recycle Bin contents, and the free space on a drive so that they are much harder to recover. It applies to every version of the App, including those distributed through the Microsoft Store. An app whose purpose is to leave no trace of what you destroyed should not keep one for us, and ProShred is built that way.

The App is identified by Microsoft Store product ID 9N4LPVVTS7RS. That ID is what this policy refers to: if the App's display name changes, this policy continues to apply to the same product without interruption.

1. We do not collect your information

ProShred does not collect, store, or share your personal information. We do not ask you to create an account, and the App does not send us your name, email address, files, file names, folder paths, or any other personal data. The only way information about you reaches us is if you choose to email us, including through "Report a problem" (see section 6).

2. Everything happens on your device

The App reads and writes your drives only to do the job you asked for, and all of that work happens entirely on your device:

  • Files overwrites the files and folders you drop or choose, then renames and deletes them.
  • Recycle Bin reads the items in your own Recycle Bin — including their original names and deletion times, so it can list them — and overwrites them when you ask.
  • Free space temporarily fills a drive's free space with its own files and then removes them. It does not open or read your existing files.
  • Shred with ProShred in the File Explorer menu passes the items you right-clicked to the App. It reads only their locations, not their contents. When a selection is too long to pass directly, the list is written to a temporary file in the App's own folder and destroyed with the same method as anything else once it has been read.

Nothing the App reads or destroys is uploaded to our servers or shared with us or any third party. Your settings are kept on your device.

3. What the App keeps on your device

  • The shred log is off by default. If you turn it on in Settings, the App records the name and location of each item it destroys, the method, the size, and the time, in a plain-text file in its own local folder. It never contains file contents. You can destroy the log from Settings at any time, and it is removed when you uninstall the App.
  • A crash log. So that a crash can be explained, the App keeps a small diagnostic log in its own local folder: the App and Windows versions, how far startup got, and the errors it met. Paths to files and folders, and your Windows user name, are removed before anything is written, so the log does not list what you shredded. It stays on your device unless you send it to us (see section 6).
  • Certificates of destruction (Pro). If you save one, the PDF contains the items and results of that job and any names, date, and organization you type into it. To suggest a name for "Performed by," the App reads your Windows account's display name on your device, and it remembers the last name you used. The PDF is saved only where you choose; the App does not keep a copy.

4. Digital signatures

If you choose to sign a certificate of destruction, the App lists suitable certificates from your own Windows certificate store and asks Windows to sign the PDF with the one you pick. The private key stays in Windows (or on your smart card or security key); the App never reads or exports it, and Windows may show its own PIN prompt. The signed PDF contains your certificate, as any digitally signed document does, so anyone you share it with can see the name and issuer on it. The App remembers which certificate you used last, by its thumbprint, so it can offer it again.

5. File access and permissions

The App declares the Microsoft Store capability that lets it reach any file you point it at, because a shredder that could only reach files picked through a file dialog could not accept items from File Explorer or a drag and drop. It uses that access only on the items you choose, and it refuses to touch Windows, program, and other protected locations whatever you choose. The App runs as a normal Windows program and does not ask for administrator rights.

To draw a file's icon, the App asks Windows for the icon of its file type without opening the file, so files stored in OneDrive or other cloud services are not downloaded just to be listed.

6. Network connections and "Report a problem"

ProShred contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking software of any kind. It makes only the following connections:

  • Microsoft Store, on your device, to check your Pro license, to process a purchase you start, and to open the Store's review dialog if you choose to rate the App.
  • A small remote settings file. Once per launch, the App downloads a tiny settings file (a few dozen bytes, such as how often to show a rating prompt) from a Google Firebase server. The request carries no account, device, or advertising identifier and nothing about your files; like any web request, it does reveal your IP address to the server that answers it. The App works normally if the request fails or is blocked.

Report a problem in Settings does not send anything by itself. It prepares a text file containing the crash log described in section 3, copies it to your clipboard, and opens a new email to feedback@gemiry.com in your own email app. Nothing reaches us unless you paste it and send the email. If you do, we receive that email, your email address, and the log, and we use them only to answer you and to diagnose and fix the problem. We keep them no longer than needed for that, and we delete them on request.

Separately, Microsoft may collect information such as downloads, purchases, and crash reports through the Microsoft Store and Windows under its own Privacy Statement. That collection is outside our control and is not something we request or receive in identifiable form.

7. Purchases

Pro is sold as a one-time Microsoft Store add-on. The purchase is handled by the Microsoft Store using your Microsoft account; we never see your payment details. The App only learns whether your account holds a valid license, which it uses to unlock Pro features.

8. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and because the App sends us nothing about you or your files, we have nothing to share. An email you choose to send us is used only as described in section 6. We may disclose information where required by law, though in practice we hold none beyond such emails.

9. Worldwide availability and your rights

The App is available worldwide, and this policy applies to you wherever you use it. Privacy laws such as the GDPR (European Economic Area and United Kingdom), the UK Data Protection Act, the CCPA/CPRA (California), the PIPL (China), and similar laws elsewhere give you rights over the personal data a company holds about you — access, correction, deletion, and portability. Apart from any email you have sent us, we hold no personal data about you. To exercise these rights over an email you sent, contact us at the address below. Your files, logs, certificates, and settings remain on your device and under your control, and you can remove them at any time by deleting them or uninstalling the App.

10. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

12. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@gemiry.com.
