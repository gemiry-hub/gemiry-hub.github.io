Privacy Policy for FilmScan

Last updated: October 30, 2026
Microsoft Store product ID: 9P7JT9HFM32G

This Privacy Policy explains how Gemiry Company Limited ("Gemiry," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with Gemiry FilmScan ("FilmScan" or the "App"), a Windows app that turns scanned film negatives into photos and lets you edit their colour. It applies to every version of the App, including those distributed through the Microsoft Store. Your photos are your own, and FilmScan works on them without sending them anywhere.

The App is identified by Microsoft Store product ID 9P7JT9HFM32G. That ID is what this policy refers to: if the App's display name changes, this policy continues to apply to the same product without interruption.

1. We do not collect your information

FilmScan does not collect, store, or share your personal information. We do not ask you to create an account, and the App does not send us your name, email address, scans, photos, file names, folder paths, or any other personal data. The only way information about you reaches us is if you choose to email us, including through "Report a problem" (see section 5).

2. Your scans and photos stay on your device

Everything the App does with your images happens entirely on your device:

  • Open negatives reads the image files or folder you choose.
  • Scan asks your film scanner for an image, through its Windows driver or directly over USB, and holds each scan in the App's own temporary folder until you save it. Temporary scans are removed when you close the App.
  • Watching a folder reads new image files that your scanner's own software saves into the folder you pick, and nothing else in it.
  • Editing and Save photos convert and adjust the images on your device and write the results only where you choose. If "Include film details" is on, each saved photo records the film stock, camera, lens, ISO, and date you entered for the roll in its own file metadata, which anyone you share the photo with can read. With it off, the photo records only that it was made with FilmScan.

Nothing the App reads, scans, or saves is uploaded to our servers or shared with us or any third party.

3. What the App keeps on your device

  • Settings and recent folders. Your preferences, the scanner you used last, and a short list of folders you opened recently, so you can open them again. You can clear the list in the App.
  • A scan log. So that a scanner problem can be explained, the App records each scan attempt and each scanner check: the scanner's name and model, the settings asked for and the ones the driver used, how long each stage took, and how it ended. It never contains images.
  • A crash log. The App and Windows versions, how far startup got, and the errors it met. An error message can include a file path, and a Windows file path usually contains your Windows user name.

These logs are plain text in the App's own local folder, are limited in size, and stay on your device unless you send them to us (see section 5). They are removed when you uninstall the App.

4. Scanners and permissions

FilmScan runs as a normal desktop program and does not ask for administrator rights. It reads the files and folders you choose and talks to the scanners connected to your PC; it does not use your camera, microphone, location, or contacts.

Some film scanners have no Windows driver. For those, the App can install a small driver package that ships inside the App and uses Windows' own USB scanner driver. Windows asks your permission before installing it, it is never installed over a scanner maker's own driver, and you can remove it from Settings at any time. Installing it downloads nothing.

5. Network connections and "Report a problem"

FilmScan contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking software of any kind. Its only connection is to the Microsoft Store, on your device, to check your Pro license, to process a purchase you start, and to open the Store's review dialog if you choose to rate the App.

Report a problem in Settings does not send anything by itself. It prepares a text file containing the scan log and crash log described in section 3, copies it to your clipboard, and opens a new email to feedback@gemiry.com in your own email app. The file holds no scans or photos, and you can read all of it before sending. Nothing reaches us unless you paste it and send the email. If you do, we receive that email, your email address, and the logs, and we use them only to answer you and to diagnose and fix the problem. We keep them no longer than needed for that, and we delete them on request.

Separately, Microsoft may collect information such as downloads, purchases, and crash reports through the Microsoft Store and Windows under its own Privacy Statement. That collection is outside our control and is not something we request or receive in identifiable form.

6. Purchases

Pro is sold as a one-time Microsoft Store add-on. The purchase is handled by the Microsoft Store using your Microsoft account; we never see your payment details. The App only learns whether your account holds a valid license, which it uses to unlock Pro features.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and because the App sends us nothing about you or your photos, we have nothing to share. An email you choose to send us is used only as described in section 5. We may disclose information where required by law, though in practice we hold none beyond such emails.

8. Worldwide availability and your rights

The App is available worldwide, and this policy applies to you wherever you use it. Privacy laws such as the GDPR (European Economic Area and United Kingdom), the UK Data Protection Act, the CCPA/CPRA (California), the PIPL (China), and similar laws elsewhere give you rights over the personal data a company holds about you — access, correction, deletion, and portability. Apart from any email you have sent us, we hold no personal data about you. To exercise these rights over an email you sent, contact us at the address below. Your scans, photos, logs, and settings remain on your device and under your control, and you can remove them at any time by deleting them or uninstalling the App.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

11. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@gemiry.com.
