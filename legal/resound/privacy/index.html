Privacy Policy for Re:Sound

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gemiry Company Limited ("Gemiry," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with Re:Sound - Audio Voice Recorder (the "App"), a Windows app that records your microphones and your PC's sound, with markers, transcripts, and a timeline editor. It applies to every version of the App, including those distributed through the Microsoft Store.

1. We do not collect your information

Re:Sound does not collect, store, or share your personal information. We do not ask you to create an account, and the App does not send your recordings, transcripts, markers, file names, usage data, or any other personal data to us or to anyone else.

2. Microphone and system sound

The App uses your microphone and your PC's sound only for the job you asked for, and all of that work happens entirely on your device:

  • Recording starts only when you press Record, and captures only the sources you switched on — microphones, system sound, or the sound of a single app you picked.
  • Level meters listen to the switched-on sources while the Record screen is open, so you can check them before recording. Nothing is stored until you press Record. Windows shows its microphone indicator while this happens.
  • Recordings, markers, and waveforms are saved to the folder you chose (Documents\Sound Recordings by default) and to the App's own local storage.

Windows asks for your permission before the App can use a microphone, and you can withdraw it at any time in Windows Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone.

3. Transcripts

Live transcripts and transcripts of saved recordings are made on your PC by speech models that run offline. No audio and no text is sent anywhere to transcribe it. Transcripts are saved with the recording in the App's library and as a text file beside the audio.

The speech models are not included in the App. When you turn transcription on, or choose to download a model, the App downloads the model file once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). That download carries no audio, no text, and no account or device identifier; like any web request, it reveals your IP address to the server that answers it, under Hugging Face's own Privacy Policy. You can remove the models at any time in Settings.

4. Network connections

Re:Sound contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking software of any kind. Besides the model download above, it makes only the following connections:

  • Microsoft Store, on your device, to check your Pro license, to process a purchase you start, and to open the Store's review dialog if you choose to rate the App.
  • A small remote settings file. Once per launch, the App downloads a tiny settings file (a few dozen bytes, such as whether to show a rating prompt) from a Google Firebase server. The request carries no account, device, or advertising identifier and nothing about your recordings; like any web request, it does reveal your IP address to the server that answers it. The App works normally if the request fails or is blocked.

Separately, Microsoft may collect information such as downloads, purchases, and crash reports through the Microsoft Store and Windows under its own Privacy Statement. That collection is outside our control and is not something we request or receive in identifiable form.

5. Reporting a problem

Settings > Report a problem puts a text file of the App's own logs on your clipboard and opens your email app with a message to us. Nothing is sent unless you paste the file and send that email yourself. The logs can contain device names (such as your microphone's name) and file paths, but never audio or transcripts. We use a report only to fix the problem it describes and delete it when it is no longer needed.

6. Sharing and recording others

When you use Share, the App hands the recording to the Windows share sheet and the app you choose; what happens next is governed by that app. When you record other people, such as a call or a meeting, you are responsible for having their consent where the law requires it.

7. Purchases

Pro is sold as Microsoft Store add-ons. The purchase is handled by the Microsoft Store using your Microsoft account; we never see your payment details. The App only learns whether your account holds a valid license, which it uses to unlock Pro features.

8. Selling and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information, and because the App sends us nothing about you or your recordings, we have nothing to share. We may disclose information where required by law, though in practice we hold none.

9. Worldwide availability and your rights

The App is available worldwide, and this policy applies to you wherever you use it. Privacy laws such as the GDPR (European Economic Area and United Kingdom), the UK Data Protection Act, the CCPA/CPRA (California), the PIPL (China), and similar laws elsewhere give you rights over the personal data a company holds about you — access, correction, deletion, and portability. We hold no personal data about you, apart from any problem report you choose to email us, which we will delete on request. Your recordings, transcripts, and settings remain on your device and under your control, and you can remove them at any time by deleting them or uninstalling the App.

10. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

12. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@gemiry.com.
