Privacy Policy for Duplicate Cleaner

Last updated: September 15, 2026
Microsoft Store product ID: 9PD4B4BHDJJS

This Privacy Policy explains how Gemiry Company Limited ("Gemiry," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with Duplicate Cleaner (the "App"), an app that finds and removes duplicate files and similar photos on your device. It applies to both editions of the App: Duplicate Cleaner for Android, distributed through Google Play, and Duplicate Cleaner for Windows, distributed through the Microsoft Store. We built Duplicate Cleaner with privacy in mind.

The two editions differ in one respect that matters for privacy: the Android edition includes Google analytics and advertising software, and the Windows edition includes none. Where a section applies to only one edition, it says so.

On Windows, the App is identified by Microsoft Store product ID 9PD4B4BHDJJS. That ID is what this policy refers to: if the App's display name changes, this policy continues to apply to the same product without interruption.

1. We do not collect your personal information

Duplicate Cleaner does not collect, store, or share your personal information. We do not ask you to create an account, and the App does not gather your name, email address, contacts, files, file names, folder paths, or other personal data to send to us.

2. Your files stay on your device

Scanning, comparing, and deleting files happens entirely on your device. The App reads your photos, videos, audio, documents, and other files only to detect duplicates and similar photos, and nothing it reads is uploaded to our servers or shared with any third party. Files are removed only when you choose to remove them. Any settings or preferences you configure are kept on your device.

On Windows, the App also:

  • Sends removed files to the Recycle Bin by default, so you can restore them from Windows if you change your mind.
  • If you enable the Pro backup folder, moves removed files to a folder you pick instead, together with a plain-text manifest and a short "how to restore" note, so they can be put back in their original folders later. That folder is yours: it stays where you put it, on your device or drive, and is never sent anywhere.
  • Skips Windows, Program Files, and app-data folders, and does not download cloud-only OneDrive or Dropbox files just to check them.
  • Keeps a small diagnostics log in its own local app-data folder to help diagnose crashes. The log stays on your device and reaches us only if you choose to send it to us yourself.

3. Permissions

Android. To do its job, the App asks for permission to access media and files on your device (for example, photos, videos, audio, or all-files access, depending on your Android version). These permissions are used solely to scan for and remove duplicates. You can revoke them at any time in Android Settings, though the App will not be able to scan without them.

Windows. The App runs as a normal desktop program under your own user account and does not run as administrator. It reads the folders and drives you choose to scan, and nothing else is opened. The App is packaged for the Microsoft Store with the standard full-trust desktop capability, which is what lets an ordinary Windows program open the files you point it at.

4. Analytics and advertising (Android edition only)

The Android edition of the App uses the following Google services, which may collect anonymous usage and device information such as app events, crash reports, device model, operating system version, approximate (country-level) location, and advertising identifiers. The Windows edition contains none of these services (see section 5).

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — anonymous usage statistics and crash reports that help us understand how the App is used and fix problems.
  • Google AdMob — to display ads in the App. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted.

In the terms used by Google Play's data-safety label, the SDKs may collect the following, all for the purpose of analytics:

Data category Data type Purpose
App activity App interactions (which features are used, app opens) Analytics
Device or other IDs A random, per-install app instance ID Analytics
Location Approximate (country-level) location derived from IP address Analytics
Financial info Purchase history (that an in-app purchase happened; no payment details) Analytics

This information is collected and processed by Google under its own privacy policy. We never send your files or file contents to these services. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

You can limit ad personalization or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

5. Network connections (Windows edition)

The Windows edition of Duplicate Cleaner contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking software of any kind. It makes only the following connections:

  • Microsoft Store, on your device, to check your Pro license, to process a purchase you start, and to open the Store's review dialog if you choose to rate the App. If a photo format needs a Windows image extension the App does not have, it can open that extension's Store page for you; nothing is installed without you.
  • A small remote settings file. Once per launch, the App downloads a tiny settings file (a few dozen bytes, such as whether to show a rating prompt) from a Google Firebase server. The request carries no account, device, or advertising identifier and nothing about your files; like any web request, it does reveal your IP address to the server that answers it. The App works normally if the request fails or is blocked.

Separately, Microsoft may collect information such as downloads, purchases, and crash reports through the Microsoft Store and Windows under its own Privacy Statement. That collection is outside our control and is not something we request or receive in identifiable form.

6. Purchases

Pro features are sold as in-app purchases: through Google Play on Android, and as a one-time Microsoft Store add-on on Windows. In both cases the purchase is handled by the store using your Google or Microsoft account, and we never see your payment details. The App only learns whether your account holds a valid license, which it uses to unlock Pro features.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. On Android, the only information leaving the App is the anonymous analytics and advertising data described in section 4, which goes to Google as our service provider. On Windows, nothing about you leaves your device beyond the connections described in section 5. We may also disclose information where required by law, though in practice we hold none.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Worldwide availability and your rights

The App is available worldwide, and this policy applies to you wherever you use it. Privacy laws such as the GDPR (European Economic Area and United Kingdom), the UK Data Protection Act, the CCPA/CPRA (California), the PIPL (China), and similar laws elsewhere give you rights over the personal data a company holds about you — access, correction, deletion, and portability. Because we do not collect personal information ourselves, such requests generally concern data held by Google (Android edition) or Microsoft; you can also uninstall the App to stop any further collection. Your files, backups, and settings remain on your device and under your control. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

11. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@gemiry.com.
